How to Build Your Finance Empire

Start with $100 and a small tax prep office. Buy businesses, stack upgrades, hire staff, and prestige for permanent multipliers. The goal: reach the top of the leaderboard.

Prestige — The Reset That Compounds

The Prestige button lights up in the header when your Net Worth (the stat bar, 3rd tile) reaches the threshold for your current level. Net Worth is your all-time total earnings — not your current cash.

When you prestige, your cash and firms reset to zero — but you earn a permanent income multiplier that doubles with every prestige. Each run gets faster than the last.
